Food goes off because we forget it's there

The milk at the back of the shelf, the spinach you meant to use on Tuesday. FridgeFit makes what you already own visible, and turns it into dinner before it turns into waste.

  1. Add your food

    Scan a receipt, take a photo of your fridge, or add items by hand.

  2. See what's expiring

    Items are sorted by how soon they go off, so the urgent ones are always on top.

  3. Cook it first

    Get recipes that use what you have, starting with what needs using today.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built around one question: what can I make with what I already have?

My Fridge

Your pantry in one place, with quantities, storage location and expiry dates, plus reminders before anything goes off.

Smart Scan

Photograph a shopping receipt or the inside of your fridge, and FridgeFit suggests the items to add. You confirm before anything is saved.

Discover

Online recipes matched to your pantry, ranked so the ones using soon-to-expire food come first, with a clear list of what you have and what's missing.

Saved Recipes

Keep the recipes you like, photos included, stored on your iPhone so they're there even offline.

Smart substitutions

Missing an ingredient? Get suggestions for what to use instead, generated on your device with Apple Intelligence.

Impact & Budget

See how much food you've used versus wasted, and keep an eye on what your groceries cost.

Private by design

Your pantry and saved recipes live on your iPhone. Receipt reading and ingredient substitutions run on-device. When you search for online recipes, only ingredient names are sent to our recipe provider.
