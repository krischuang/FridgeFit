Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: your pantry, saved recipes and budget stay on your iPhone. Receipt reading and ingredient substitutions run on your device. We store only what's needed for your account and Camera Mode. We have no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

This policy explains what information the FridgeFit iOS app ("FridgeFit", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. FridgeFit was built by a student team at the University of Technology Sydney.

1. Information stored only on your iPhone

The following is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your pantry: food items, quantities, storage locations, expiry dates and activity history
  • Recipes you save from Discover, including their photos
  • Your budget, grocery spending, item prices and food waste records (Impact & Budget)
  • Your Discover preferences (diet, intolerances) and recipes you've hidden

Deleting the app deletes this information.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Camera and photos. When you scan a receipt or photograph your fridge, the image is analysed on your iPhone to suggest items. Images are not uploaded or kept by FridgeFit.
  • Apple Intelligence. Receipt interpretation and ingredient substitutions use Apple's on-device model. Your receipts, pantry and recipes are not sent to us or to any AI service for these features.

3. Information we store with your account

FridgeFit requires an account. It uses Google Firebase to provide sign-in and Camera Mode pairing, and stores only the information below. Your pantry, recipes and budget are not stored with your account.

InformationWhy
Email address, name and profile photo (from email sign-in or Sign in with Google), and an account IDTo create your account and sign you in
Device name, an app-specific device ID, its role (camera or viewer), and connection statusTo pair your own iPhones for Camera Mode
Temporary connection details for a Camera Mode sessionTo connect your two iPhones. The video is sent directly between your devices; if a direct connection isn't possible it may pass through an encrypted relay. It is never recorded or stored.

4. Information shared with third parties

  • Spoonacular (recipe search). When you use Discover, FridgeFit sends your search criteria to Spoonacular to find recipes: the names of ingredients in your pantry, and any diet and intolerance filters you've chosen. Quantities, expiry dates and budget figures are not sent. No account details are sent. Recipe images are loaded from Spoonacular's servers. As with any web request, Spoonacular receives your IP address. Spoonacular’s own privacy policy is available on its website.
  • Google Firebase (authentication and database) stores the account and pairing information in section 3. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it, shares your name, email and profile photo with FridgeFit. See Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites. FridgeFit contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan receipts, photograph your fridge, and stream a live view in Camera Mode.
  • Local Network: Camera Mode lets you use a second iPhone, signed in to your own account, as a camera pointed at your fridge. When both iPhones are on the same Wi-Fi, this permission lets the video travel directly between them instead of over the internet. FridgeFit doesn't scan or access other devices on your network.

Both are asked for only when you first use the feature, and you can change them at any time in Settings → FridgeFit.

6. How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it, delete your account, or remove the app. Account and pairing information is kept while your account exists, and Camera Mode connection details are only needed for the length of a session. Deleting your account in the app removes it immediately; requests made by email are completed within 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • An account is needed to use FridgeFit, but beyond your sign-in details and device pairing, your kitchen data stays on your iPhone.
  • Edit or delete pantry items, saved recipes and budget records in the app.
  • Delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of its data, on the device and in Firebase.
  • Ask for a copy of, or a correction to, your account data by emailing us from the address you signed in with.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Security

Account data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only your signed-in account can read it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

FridgeFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: fridgefit.help@outlook.com
