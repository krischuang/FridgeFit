Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: your pantry and saved recipes stay on your iPhone. Receipt reading and ingredient substitutions run on your device. We store only what's needed for your account, budget and remote camera. We have no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains what information the FridgeFit iOS app ("FridgeFit", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. FridgeFit was built by a student team at the University of Technology Sydney.

1. Information stored only on your iPhone

The following is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your pantry: food items, quantities, storage locations, expiry dates and activity history
  • Recipes you save from Discover, including their photos
  • Your Discover preferences (diet, intolerances) and recipes you've hidden

Deleting the app deletes this information.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Camera and photos. When you scan a receipt or photograph your fridge, the image is analysed on your iPhone to suggest items. Images are not uploaded or kept by FridgeFit.
  • Apple Intelligence. Receipt interpretation and ingredient substitutions use Apple's on-device model. Your receipts, pantry and recipes are not sent to us or to any AI service for these features.

3. Information we store with your account

If you sign in, FridgeFit uses Google Firebase to provide your account and the features below. This information is linked to your account.

InformationWhy
Email address, name and profile photo (from email sign-in or Sign in with Google), and an account IDTo create your account and sign you in
Budget, spending, item prices and food waste records you enterTo show your Impact & Budget figures
Device name, an app-specific device ID, its role (camera or viewer), and connection statusTo pair your own iPhones for the remote camera
Temporary connection details for a remote camera sessionTo connect your two iPhones. The video itself is sent directly between your devices and is not stored.

4. Information shared with third parties

  • Spoonacular (recipe search). When you use Discover, FridgeFit sends the names of ingredients in your pantry, plus any diet or intolerance filters you've chosen, to Spoonacular to find recipes. No account details are sent. Recipe images are loaded from Spoonacular's servers. As with any web request, Spoonacular receives your IP address. Spoonacular’s own privacy policy is available on its website.
  • Google Firebase (authentication and database) stores the account information in section 3. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it, shares your name, email and profile photo with FridgeFit. See Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites. FridgeFit contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan receipts, photograph your fridge, and act as a remote camera. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → FridgeFit.

6. How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Account information is kept while your account exists, and remote camera connection details are only needed for the length of a session. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete its data within 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use FridgeFit's pantry, recipes and on-device features without sharing anything with us.
  • Edit or delete pantry items, saved recipes and budget records in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of, a correction to, or deletion of your account data by emailing us from the address you signed in with.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Security

Account data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only your signed-in account can read it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

FridgeFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
