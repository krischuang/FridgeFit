Frequently asked questions

Getting started

How do I add food to My Fridge?

Open My Fridge to add an item by hand, or use Smart Scan to read a shopping receipt or a photo of your fridge. Scanned items are shown for you to review, and nothing is added until you confirm.

Do I need an account?

Yes. FridgeFit uses a free account (email or Sign in with Google) to identify you and to pair your own iPhones for Camera Mode. Your pantry, saved recipes and budget are stored on your iPhone, not in your account.

Recipes and Discover

Why does Discover show no recipes, or say it can't connect?

Discover searches an online recipe service, so it needs an internet connection. The service also has a daily request limit. If it has been reached, you may see recently cached results until the limit resets the next day. Your saved recipes always work offline.

Discover says an ingredient is missing, but I have it.

FridgeFit compares the recipe's ingredient names with the names in My Fridge. It handles plurals ("egg" / "eggs") and descriptions like "large" or "mild", but it won't treat different foods as the same ("brown sugar" is not "sugar"). Renaming the item in My Fridge to match the recipe usually fixes it. Items with a quantity of zero count as missing.

I added food to My Fridge. Do I need to refresh Discover?

No. When you return to Discover, what you have and what's missing are re-checked against your current fridge automatically. Use refresh only when you want a new set of recipes.

A recipe has missing steps or a broken photo.

Some recipes from our provider are incomplete. Use Hide on the recipe and it won't appear again. You can bring hidden recipes back from Discover at any time.

Are saved recipes available offline?

Yes. Saving a recipe from Discover stores it, with its photo, on your iPhone.

Apple Intelligence features

Ingredient substitutions or receipt reading aren't available.

These features use Apple Intelligence on your device. They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. The model may also need a few minutes to finish downloading after you turn it on.

Remote camera

My second iPhone won't connect as a camera.

Both iPhones must be signed in to the same FridgeFit account, connected to the internet, and have FridgeFit open. In Settings → FridgeFit, allow Camera on the iPhone used as the camera and Local Network on both, so they can stream directly when they're on the same Wi-Fi.

Your data

How do I delete my data or my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, review what will be deleted, confirm it's you, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your pantry, history, saved recipes, budget and sign-in, and signs you out. It can't be undone.

Deleting the app alone only removes the data on that iPhone; your account remains until you delete it in the app. If you can't sign in, email us from the address you registered with and we'll delete it for you. See our Privacy Policy for details.

System requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 26 or later
  • Internet connection for Discover, sign-in and Camera Mode
  • An Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone for ingredient substitutions and receipt reading

Report a problem

Email us with the details above. If FridgeFit crashed, tell us what you were doing just before. You can also share a crash report from Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data.
